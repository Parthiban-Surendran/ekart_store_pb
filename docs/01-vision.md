# Ekart Vision

## Mission

To build India's most trusted online platform for purchasing pooja products by providing customers with authentic products, fast delivery, and a seamless shopping experience.

---

## Vision

To become the leading digital marketplace for pooja stores, temples, and spiritual products across India.

---

## Target Customers

- Individual Customers
- Families
- Temples
- Pooja Stores
- Event Organizers
- Wholesale Buyers

---

## Problems We Solve

- Difficult to find authentic pooja products
- No organized online pooja marketplace
- No trusted local pooja store platform
- Lack of doorstep delivery
- No easy comparison between products

---

## Business Goals

- Sell pooja products online
- Support multiple vendors in future
- Build customer trust
- Increase repeat customers
- Expand across India

---

## Future Vision

- Mobile Application
- Multi-language Support
- Temple Booking
- Subscription Orders
- AI Product Recommendations
- WhatsApp Ordering
- Vendor Portal
